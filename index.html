Welcome to LimiPlake — where learning meets creativity.

LimiPlake is a home for explorers who love both music and coding. You can learn to play piano, write programs, and build your imagination one idea at a time. Whether you’re composing a tune or creating your first app, we’re here to help you grow, play, and make something amazing.

Once upon a time, I made a poster in kindergarten that said, “When I grow up, I want to be a piano teacher.” That dream stayed with me — and in the summer of 2021–2022, I started a small club. I wanted to share both my favorite things: music and programming. After the summer break, I renamed the club to PCLWA. But that name was already taken, so I changed it to PACLA. That name was taken too — and that’s when I came up with something truly original: LimiPlake, the name that means limitless learning. And that’s how LimiPlake began — from a dream on a poster to a place where everyone can learn from the first note to the last line of code.
